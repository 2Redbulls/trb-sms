This Privacy Policy explains how TRB (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS/text messaging program.

1. Information we collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from TRB, we may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your name and contact details you provide
  • Message consent / opt-in records and opt-out requests
  • Message delivery metadata (for example, timestamps and delivery status)
  • Message content you send to us (for example, STOP or HELP replies)

2. How we use your information

We use this information to:

  • Send transactional notifications and operational alerts you requested or consented to receive
  • Confirm opt-in, process HELP requests, and honor STOP / opt-out requests
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the messaging program
  • Comply with carrier, industry, and legal requirements for A2P messaging

Message frequency varies based on your activity and the alerts you request. Message and data rates may apply.

3. Mobile information and consent — no third-party sharing for marketing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers strictly to deliver and operate the messaging program (for example, Twilio as our SMS platform). Those providers are engaged to process information on our behalf for program operation, not to buy, rent, or market your SMS consent.

4. How we obtain consent

You opt in by providing your mobile number and giving clear verbal or written consent to receive SMS from TRB before messages are sent. We do not buy or rent contact lists for this program.

5. Opt-out and help

  • Reply STOP to cancel. You will receive a confirmation and no further program messages will be sent unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email mrfrom@gmail.com.

6. Data retention

We keep messaging consent records and related logs as needed to operate the program, honor opt-outs, and meet legal/carrier requirements, then delete or de-identify them when no longer needed.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children’s privacy

This messaging program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 via SMS.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we post updates to this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the SMS program: mrfrom@gmail.com.
